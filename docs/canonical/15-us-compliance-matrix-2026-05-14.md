# US Compliance Matrix (Customer-Facing) — 2026-05-14

> **Audience.** Sales, security review, and procurement teams at customers and prospects, plus auditors who need a single artifact mapping every US compliance claim Nyayavigya makes to the code, configuration, or policy that enforces it.
>
> **Scope.** US federal statutes, state privacy / breach laws, civil-procedure & IP statutes, ethics rules, and security standards that the Nyayavigya platform implements today. Companion to canonical doc `13-us-compliance-status-2026-05-13.md` (engineering-internal status crosscheck) and the auditor-facing `docstotrack/A-active/05-audit-and-security/NYV-COMPLIANCE-ATTESTATION.md` (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 control map).
>
> **What this doc is not.** A gap analysis. Items not listed here are not silently in scope — the gap analysis is a separate, in-flight task.

## How enforcement works (read first)

Every Nyayavigya request traverses a fixed safety ring before any model call is dispatched: **tenant isolation → guardrails → jurisdiction binding → policy / consent gates → orchestration → PII redaction → LLM router → response engineering → audit hash-chain → streaming**. The jurisdiction-binding middleware (`src/middleware/nv-jurisdiction-binding-middleware.ts`) pins currency, court hierarchy, statute terminology, and the mandatory AI-output disclosure to every US request. The policy engine (`src/services/compliance/nv-policy-engine.ts`) seeds and enforces a row in `nv_policies` for each US framework below; pre-flight middleware (e.g. `nv-bipa-consent-gate.ts`) blocks requests that violate a gating policy with a structured 403 quoting the policy code. Every accepted request, every consent grant/revoke, every breach notice, and every policy-enforcement decision appends a tamper-evident record to the `nv_audit_logs` hash chain through the single `appendAuditRecord` writer (`src/middleware/nv-sdk-tracking-middleware.ts:398-505`), so the compliance evidence trail can be reconstructed deterministically end-to-end.
